Founder & Creator
The woman behind the brand

A mother. A survivor. A student. An entrepreneur.

A woman who refuses to give up.

Welcome to Scruffy Butt Digital Designs. I am the creator and founder behind this growing digital business, but my story began long before I dreamed of becoming an entrepreneur.

My past is part of my story. My future is what I'm building.

I am a proud single mother of three beautiful children, a survivor of childhood sexual abuse and domestic violence, a college student pursuing a degree in Computer Science, and a woman determined to turn a lifetime of struggles into something meaningful.

My journey has been anything but easy. I have faced betrayal, heartbreak, financial hardship, homelessness, and moments when simply making it through another day felt like an accomplishment. Through every painful experience and setback, I discovered that my circumstances may have shaped parts of my story, but they do not get to determine how my story ends.

💕 Motherhood: My Greatest Motivation

Being a single mother of three has been both my greatest blessing and one of my biggest challenges. Raising children while navigating personal trauma, financial instability, and the responsibilities of everyday life has tested me in ways I never imagined.

My children are the reason I continue pushing forward. I want them to grow up knowing that their mother never stopped trying, that where they come from does not determine where they can go, and that education creates opportunity. I want to create a future filled with stability and security.

🎓 Education: Turning Dreams Into Reality

I am currently pursuing a college education in Computer Science with dreams of building a successful career in cybersecurity. Technology represents independence, opportunity, financial stability, and the ability to make a difference.

Having experienced hardships involving privacy, security, and trust, I understand how devastating it can feel when your sense of safety is compromised. Those experiences inspire my interest in digital protection, cybersecurity, identifying security threats, and helping prevent cybercrime.

💻 How Scruffy Butt Digital Designs Was Born

Scruffy Butt Digital Designs grew from my passion for creativity, technology, personal growth, and entrepreneurship. I wanted to build something that belonged to me while continuing my education and raising my children.

What started as an idea became a vision for an online digital bookstore and creative marketplace offering digital books, educational materials, self-improvement resources, budgeting guides, financial literacy content, creative products, and tools that encourage people to invest in themselves.

This business represents rebuilding my life, creating financial independence, and transforming painful experiences into motivation for something greater.

🚀 My Vision for the Future

I envision growing this website into a thriving digital marketplace supporting education, financial independence, creativity, entrepreneurship, and personal development. As my experience grows, I plan to expand into website development, digital marketing, cybersecurity education, and automation.

One of my biggest dreams is to use my experiences to encourage other survivors of abuse and domestic violence, especially single mothers who may feel trapped by their circumstances. I want them to know that their past does not disqualify them from having a successful future.

🦋 More Than a Survivor

I refuse to let the worst things that happened to me become the only things that define me. I am also a mother raising three children, a college student pursuing her dreams, a creative thinker, a determined entrepreneur, and a woman who believes that something extraordinary can come from an incredibly difficult beginning.

I am still learning, still healing, still growing, and still building. Every class I complete, every new skill I learn, every improvement to my website, and every step toward growing my business represents progress.

❤️ Thank You for Being Part of My Journey

When you visit Scruffy Butt Digital Designs, explore my digital bookstore, purchase a product, share my website, or support my small business, you are supporting a mother's dream of building a better future for her children and a survivor determined to keep moving forward.

Thank you for believing in my vision and supporting Scruffy Butt Digital Designs.
