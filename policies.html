Our Commitment

Scruffy Butt Digital Designs provides educational resources, personalized graphic designs, digital products, and creative solutions with transparency, customer satisfaction, intellectual property protection, and respect for customer privacy and ownership rights.

Customers must review and accept the applicable terms before completing a purchase or entering a service agreement.

1. Website Terms & Conditions

1.1 Website Use

Visitors may browse products, purchase digital resources, request services, explore completed designs, and contact the business. Users may not engage in unlawful conduct, interfere with website operations, attempt unauthorized access, or misuse products and services.

1.2 Availability

Products and services are subject to availability. Scruffy Butt Digital Designs may modify offerings, pricing, or availability and may decline requests that cannot reasonably or legally be fulfilled.

1.3 Accuracy & Third Parties

We strive to provide accurate product descriptions and pricing. Website availability may be affected by maintenance or technical issues. External payment, social media, fundraising, and other third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

2. Copyright Ownership, AI-Generated Designs & Liability

2.1 Business Copyright Protection

The business retains applicable rights in original digital books, website content, branding, logos, promotional graphics, and artwork not otherwise transferred. Unauthorized reproduction, resale, redistribution, modification, or commercial exploitation is prohibited unless written permission is granted.

2.2 Personalized Design Ownership

Once full payment clears and the Copyright Transfer Agreement is signed by the rights holder, the customer owns the paid-for final custom design or custom digital product and the transferable copyright rights identified in that agreement. Scruffy Butt Digital Designs will not reuse, resell, license, or publicly display that work. The customer may separately sign a Gallery and Portfolio Display Release to allow display of the approved design in the Scruffy Butt gallery.

2.3 Artificial Intelligence Disclosure

AI tools, generative platforms, image-generation software, automated editing, and other creative technology may assist with concepts, illustrations, photo enhancement, backgrounds, modifications, and digital product designs. AI-assisted content may have technological, copyright, trademark, or licensing limitations. Copyright eligibility for work containing AI output depends on the human-authored expression in the work. Project-specific disclosures and usage terms belong in the signed agreement. U.S. Copyright Office guidance on AI and copyrightability

2.4 Liability & Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for lawful use, submitted materials, permissions, modifications, and uses outside granted rights. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the business disclaims certain indirect or consequential damages arising from customer misconduct or uses outside agreed rights. These limits do not exclude liability that cannot legally be disclaimed.

2.5 Third-Party Tools & Exclusivity

Third-party platforms operate under separate licenses and terms. Scruffy Butt Digital Designs does not guarantee copyright eligibility, trademark availability, or exclusivity for AI-generated elements or unprotectable content.

2.6 Personal use, commercial use, and copyright transfer

Your order or written agreement states which rights you receive. Personal-use licenses cover the agreed personal use. Commercial licenses identify permitted business uses, such as marketing, merchandise, or resale where expressly allowed. A copyright assignment applies only to eligible rights the seller owns and expressly transfers. Third-party asset licenses continue to apply.

2.7 Infringement & Enforcement

The business may investigate suspected infringement and request removal or pursue appropriate legal remedies. Copyright complaints may be submitted through official contact channels.

3. Personalized Design Services

3.1 Custom Requests

Requests may include personalized graphics, logos, photo collages, merchandise artwork, invitations, social media graphics, and business materials. Requests should identify the customer, description, colors, text, photos, intended use, deadline, and estimated budget.

3.2 Project Approval

A project is accepted only after scope, availability, price, delivery terms, and applicable ownership rights are confirmed. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.

3.3 Revisions & Approval

The number of included revisions appears in the project quote or agreement. Additional revisions or substantial changes may require additional payment. Final approval confirms acceptance of agreed deliverables.

3.4 Buying a custom digital design

  1. Submit your brief with the intended use, file needs, and reference images you have permission to share.
  2. Review and accept the written quote and agreement, including the deliverables, revision limits, payment schedule, and usage rights.
  3. Pay the deposit or first installment if your quote requires one. Review drafts and send revisions within the agreed schedule.
  4. Approve the final design and pay the remaining balance as agreed. We will deliver the digital files specified in your quote.

3.5 Delivery & Abandoned Projects

Unless the written quote specifies a different deadline, custom designs have a 72-calendar-hour delivery window after we confirm that the project is accepted, required materials are received, and any required initial payment has cleared. See the Custom Design Delivery Schedule. Final artwork is delivered in stated formats; editable source files are included only when agreed. Projects inactive for 30 consecutive days may be canceled or rescheduled according to the signed agreement.

4. Completed Designs Gallery & Portfolio

Your finished custom design stays out of our gallery unless you sign the separate Gallery and Portfolio Display Release. Choosing to keep it private does not affect your price, delivery, or ownership.

A signed release gives us limited permission to display only the approved design in our gallery. You keep ownership. The release does not authorize resale, reuse in other projects, social media posts, or advertising.

Customers may request removal of business-controlled gallery and promotional displays. Verified requests are aimed for completion within seven business days. Previously distributed materials and third-party reposts or search caches may remain outside the business's control.

5. Private Designs & Confidentiality

Customers may request confidential handling of personalized artwork and additional privacy protection for sensitive photographs or business concepts.

A written confidentiality agreement must identify customer information, covered artwork, display restrictions, permitted disclosures, legal exceptions, signatures, and dates.

Customer photographs are used only for authorized project purposes. Customers submitting photographs of minors must have the required legal authority. Confidential artwork will not be publicly displayed or promoted without subsequent written authorization, except as permitted or required by law.

6. Deposits, Payment Plans, Refunds & Cancellations

6.1 Checkout and deposits

Digital books and other ready-to-download products generally require full payment before delivery. If a custom project requires a deposit, the written quote will state the deposit amount, when it is due, how it applies to the total, when work can begin, and how cancellation or a refund affects it.

6.2 Project payment plans

A custom project may qualify for installment payments if we agree to them in writing. Before you accept, the agreement will state the total price, installment amounts and due dates, any applicable fees, and when final files or agreed rights are released. If a third-party financing option is offered, review that provider’s separate terms before accepting.

6.3 Refunds

Digital purchases are generally final after successful delivery or access, subject to applicable law and payment-provider requirements. Assistance or refunds may be available for defective files, incorrect products, duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, or materially misrepresented products.

6.4 Custom Cancellations

Customers may request cancellation before work begins. After work begins, refunds may be adjusted for completed services, agreed expenses, and the signed contract.

6.5 Disputes & Late Payments

Customers are encouraged to contact the business about billing concerns. This policy does not restrict lawful payment disputes or consumer protections. Overdue payments may delay or suspend work as permitted by the applicable agreement.

7. Digital Bookstore & Product Licensing

The bookstore offers downloadable books, guides, templates, and educational resources. Unless otherwise stated, customers receive a limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable personal-use license. Purchasing a digital product does not automatically transfer copyright ownership.

Customers may not resell, redistribute, reproduce for unauthorized commercial purposes, or claim authorship of protected products. Commercial or resale rights require separate licensing terms.

Future revisions or updated editions are not guaranteed unless expressly included. Financial, cryptocurrency, business, legal, and cybersecurity materials provide general education and do not replace professional advice or guarantee outcomes.

8. Privacy & Customer Data Protection

Depending on use, the business may collect names, email addresses, contact submissions, transaction information, project descriptions, submitted images, signed agreements, and communication records.

Information is used to process orders, provide services, deliver products, document agreements, respond to inquiries, and meet legal obligations. Customer information is not sold and may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary and legally permitted.

Payments should be processed through established third-party providers; payment card numbers should not be submitted through ordinary contact or design request forms. Customer information is retained as reasonably necessary for operations and legal recordkeeping.

Customers may request access, correction, or deletion subject to applicable law and necessary recordkeeping exceptions. Reasonable security measures are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Fundraising & Donations

Voluntary contributions may support website improvements, equipment, digital products, marketing, software, operations, and professional development.

Contributions support a privately operated for-profit business. Donations do not provide ownership, equity, profit-sharing, or guaranteed products or services unless separately agreed. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. Donation processing follows the selected platform's terms and applicable law.

10. Affiliate Program & Partnerships

The business welcomes creators, businesses, and promotional partners, subject to approval. Commission, compensation, deliverables, disclosure obligations, and promotional duties require a separate written agreement.

Partners receiving compensation or material benefits must make required advertising disclosures. Partnership does not automatically grant ownership or licensing rights in the business's branding, products, or copyright materials.

11. Future Technology & Digital Services

Future website development agreements must establish scope, pricing, hosting, domains, source files, maintenance, and intellectual property rights. Future marketing agreements must establish deliverables and budgets; no particular sales or traffic result is guaranteed.

Cybersecurity educational materials provide general information. Professional cybersecurity services are not currently offered. Future professional services will require separate agreements addressing authorization, scope, confidentiality, and data handling.

Any paid membership or subscription will disclose price, renewal, cancellation, and access terms before enrollment.

12. Copyright Complaints & Infringement Reports

Individuals may submit sufficiently detailed written complaints identifying the claimed copyrighted work, allegedly infringing material, disputed location, contact information, and basis for the claim.

Formal notices may require additional information. Applicable complaints and counter-notices will be reviewed and handled according to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act and other relevant law.

13. Customer Agreements & Legal Provisions

13.1 Required Agreements

Custom orders may require a personalized design service agreement, copyright assignment, gallery display authorization, or confidentiality/non-disclosure agreement.

Open the Customer Agreement Center for the current agreement packet. The signed project documents specify the terms and rights for your order.

13.2 Electronic Signatures & Liability

Electronic signatures may be accepted when legally valid. Any limitation of liability applies only to the extent permitted by law and does not exclude consumer rights or liability that cannot legally be limited.

13.3 Disputes & Governing Law

Customers are encouraged to contact the business before formal proceedings. Governing law is established in the applicable agreement, subject to mandatory legal protections.

13.4 Policy Modifications & Support

Policies may be updated prospectively as operations or law change. Previously executed agreements remain governed by their stated terms. Policy, purchase, refund, copyright, privacy, and confidentiality questions may be submitted through the Contact page.
